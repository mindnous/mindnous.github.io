## Advanced Recognition Capabilities

### License Plate Recognition (LPR)
State-of-the-art automatic license plate recognition for parking management, access control, and security applications.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time plate detection and recognition
- Multi-country format support
- High accuracy even in challenging conditions
- Vehicle tracking and analytics
- Integration with access control systems

### Receipt & Document Processing
Intelligent extraction of information from receipts, invoices, and business documents.

**Capabilities:**
- Itemized expense extraction
- Tax and total amount recognition
- Merchant information capture
- Date and time stamping
- Multi-currency support
