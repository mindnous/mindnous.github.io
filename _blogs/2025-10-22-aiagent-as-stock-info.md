## Flash Summary

Yes! AI Agents can be used as stock assistants. However, please, please, do your own research, and then do the research more, so that your hard-earned money are invested in well-research sectors/companies that you are confident and know, and also to avoid speculation and uncertainty (Gharar and Maysir).


## Introduction

> TLDR -> AI Agents can make mistakes, so it's best to use it as your assistant, not as the decision making. We will not be responsible for any mishap for your wrong interpretation based on this blog, and this blog is not to determine which stocks to buy, it is for research/assistant only.

As Large-Language Models AI Agent become more intelligent, many people are using it as trading/stock assistants, that helps in the quest of interpreting futures based on the current infos. However, how to do it in The Halal Way (permissible according to Islam)? To narrow down the scope a little, we will use four of the most popular AI Agents: ChatGPT-5, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 pro, and Perplexity Pro with Deepresearch.

From our brief summary on this case, it seems that **Perplexity Pro** might be the most accountable in case that we really want to go into detail, or might research things that need to be referenced so that we don't need to do self-exploration to validate the output suggestion of the AI Agent. 

Thanks for reading until this point, but hold on a sec. If you've read until this point, it means that you might have decided which AI Agent to use to research the suitable stock. But if you take a look more detail on the response from them, you will notice that:
* All AI Agents are mostly searching for the possible profitable stocks from reputable reviews or news, not based on gradual news that lead to the AI Agent to conclude that it will be profitable due to improvements or opportunities in this or that.
* Until this point, if you have already decided stocks to invest in, you are basically a gambler, not an investor. As alluded in our intro, we hope that you will do waaaay more research before deciding on the stocks to invest your hard-earned money into. So, do your own homework.
